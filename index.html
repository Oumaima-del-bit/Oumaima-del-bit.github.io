Capstone · in progress · team of 5

Casablanca Live Map for Marsa Maroc

For our Business Policy & Corporate Strategy capstone on Marsa Maroc, I proposed the Casablanca Live Map: an AI tool giving a real-time view of trucks and container clearance to reduce congestion at the port of Casablanca. The team selected it as our AI business case. I wrote the Resources & Capabilities analysis (VRIO, value chain), which found 3 gaps that shaped the solution, and we're now testing its logistics feasibility with an AI agent built at AUI.
